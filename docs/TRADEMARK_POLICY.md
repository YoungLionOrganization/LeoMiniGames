# YoungLion / LeoMiniGames Trademark and Trust-Mark Policy

**Version:** 1.0 draft  
**Date:** 10 September 2026

> This policy concerns names, logos, badges and source-identifying marks. It is separate from copyright licenses such as GPL, MIT or the YoungLion proprietary Mod License.

## 1. Covered marks

To the extent protected by applicable law, this policy applies to YoungLion-controlled source-identifying names and graphics, including:

- YoungLion;
- LeoMiniGames;
- YoungLion and LeoMiniGames logos;
- the Gold Official badge;
- the Green Native/L3 Verified badge;
- the Blue Verified badge; and
- confusingly similar successor trust marks.

Use of `®` should not occur unless the relevant mark is actually registered in the applicable jurisdiction. `™` may be used where appropriate as a claim of trademark/source-identifying rights, subject to local law.

## 2. GPL and other code licenses do not grant endorsement rights

A recipient’s right under GPL, MIT, Apache, BSD, MPL, LGPL or another software license to copy, modify or redistribute code does not by itself grant a right to falsely represent the resulting product as an official YoungLion release or to use YoungLion trust badges.

Copyright permission and trademark permission are separate.

## 3. Truthful compatibility references

Absent a separate restriction imposed by mandatory law, developers may make reasonable textual statements necessary to truthfully identify compatibility, for example:

- “Compatible with LeoMiniGames”; or
- “Plugin for LeoMiniGames”.

Such references must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement or Official/Verified status.

## 4. Forks and modified distributions

A person may exercise rights granted by the LeoMiniGames GPL license, including rights to modify and redistribute covered code.

However, a materially modified or independently operated distribution should not be presented in a way that creates a misleading impression that it is the official YoungLion/LeoMiniGames build.

Recommended practice for third-party forks:

- clearly identify the fork as unofficial;
- use a distinct product presentation where confusion is likely;
- preserve copyright and license notices required by the software license; and
- do not use trust badges without authorization.

This policy should not be applied to obstruct truthful statements about the origin of GPL-covered code.

## 5. Logos

YoungLion and LeoMiniGames logos may not be used as the primary branding of an independent product without permission.

Small factual use for commentary, review, documentation, compatibility explanation or other legally protected nominative/reference use remains subject to applicable law.

## 6. Trust badges

The following marks are security/trust signals controlled by YoungLion:

### Gold Official badge

Indicates an official YoungLion publication or other status expressly assigned by the backend.

### Green Native/L3 Verified badge

Indicates a Publisher that is verified and has designated Native/L3 permission according to current backend policy.

### Blue Verified badge

Indicates a verified Publisher according to current backend policy.

A Package manifest, screenshot, icon or Publisher-supplied asset cannot grant any of these statuses.

## 7. Prohibited badge behavior

Do not:

- copy a trust badge into a Package icon to imply backend verification;
- create a confusingly similar badge intended to deceive Users;
- state “Official”, “Verified” or “Native/L3 Verified” unless the backend currently grants that status; or
- use another Publisher’s identity or badge.

## 8. Open-source packages

An open-source license does not prevent YoungLion from operating a separate trademark policy.

A third party may redistribute GPL-covered LeoMiniGames code according to the GPL while still being required not to misrepresent a fork as official or endorsed.

## 9. Marketplace metadata

Trust status displayed in LeoMiniGames should come from backend-authoritative catalog/publisher metadata, not untrusted package manifest fields.

Legacy fields may be parsed for compatibility but should not be treated as proof of status.

## 10. Permission requests

YoungLion may publish a future brand portal or contact channel for logo, press, partnership and co-branding permissions.

## 11. Registration status and legal review

This policy does not claim that every listed mark is registered in every jurisdiction. Trademark rights, unregistered marks, nominative fair use and enforcement standards differ by country.

Before substantial commercial enforcement or international brand expansion, YoungLion should obtain jurisdiction-specific trademark advice and consider registration of its core marks in priority markets.
