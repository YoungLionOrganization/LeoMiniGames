# LeoMiniGames License History

## Source-available transition

Effective for the project edition adopting this file on or after
19 September 2026, project-owned LeoMiniGames host material is offered under:

`LicenseRef-LMG-SAPEL-1.0`

The transition is a prospective licensing decision by the relevant copyright
holder(s).

## Earlier public repository revisions

Earlier public revisions of the repository contained notices identifying
project-owned host material as `GPL-3.0-or-later`.

Changing the license offered for current/future project-owned copies does not
retroactively cancel rights already validly granted to a recipient for an
earlier copy.

Accordingly:

- current/future official editions may use the new source-available license;
- a person who lawfully obtained an earlier GPL-licensed copy may retain rights
  that applied to that copy;
- the new license must not be described as retroactively deleting those rights;
- third-party components always remain governed by their own licenses.

No release tag is required for a license grant to exist if a copy was otherwise
lawfully made available under stated terms.

## Contributor rule going forward

New external contributions should not be merged unless the contributor has
accepted the current contributor terms sufficient to preserve YoungLion's
ability to maintain, distribute, sublicense, and relicense the project.
